Tuck Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Tuck is designed to help users save articles and read them later in a clean and distraction-free experience while respecting user privacy.

Tuck does not sell personal data, require user accounts, or track browsing activity for advertising purposes.

Information We Store

Tuck stores only the information necessary for the extension to function properly.

  • Saved articles and webpage content
  • Article metadata such as titles, URLs, and reading progress
  • User preferences including themes, fonts, and reading settings
  • Favorites, archived items, and tags

How Data Is Stored

Saved content and preferences are stored locally on the user’s device using browser storage APIs.

Tuck does not require account creation and does not upload personal reading data to external servers unless explicitly stated in a future feature.

Permissions Used

Tuck uses browser permissions only to provide core functionality.

  • activeTab — Access the current webpage when the user saves an article or opens reader mode.
  • scripting — Extract readable article content from webpages.
  • storage — Save articles, preferences, and reading progress locally.
  • contextMenus — Allow saving pages or links directly from the browser context menu.
  • Host permissions — Read webpage content necessary for article extraction and reader mode.

Offline Access

Tuck supports offline reading by storing saved article content locally on the device.

Third-Party Services

Tuck does not use third-party advertising trackers or sell user data.

If future versions integrate optional external services, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Sharing

Tuck does not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect locally stored information and minimize unnecessary data access.

Children’s Privacy

Tuck is not directed toward children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect feature or policy changes.
