Privacy Policy for SERP Sheet

Last updated: May 2026

SERP Sheet is designed to export Google search result titles and URLs directly to a user-selected Google Sheet.

Data We Process

SERP Sheet may process:

  • Search keywords entered by the user
  • Google Sheet URL or Sheet ID provided by the user
  • Search settings such as country, language, pages, and UULE value
  • Search result titles and URLs collected during use

How Data Is Used

The data is used only to perform the extension’s main function:

  • Open Google search result pages
  • Collect visible result titles and URLs
  • Write the results to the user’s selected Google Sheet

Data Storage

SERP Sheet does not store user data on external servers.

Any temporary extension state is stored locally or during the browser session only, depending on the extension settings. This may include progress, keywords, and selected sheet information needed to complete the current task.

Data Sharing

SERP Sheet does not sell, share, rent, or transfer user data to third parties.

Google Sheets Access

SERP Sheet uses Google OAuth only to write results to the Google Sheet selected by the user. The extension does not access Gmail, Google Drive files, contacts, calendar, or other Google services.

Tracking and Analytics

SERP Sheet does not use tracking, analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party monitoring tools.

Security

All communication with Google services uses secure HTTPS connections. User data stays under the user’s control and is sent only to the selected Google Sheet.

Responsible Use

Users are responsible for using SERP Sheet in compliance with Google’s terms and all applicable policies.
